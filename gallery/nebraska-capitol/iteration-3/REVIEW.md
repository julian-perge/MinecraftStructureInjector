# Iteration 3 final visual review

Reviewed the final 16-image contact sheet, aerial, and full-resolution rotunda view. The model has a continuous tower/base connection, four enclosed gardens, recognizable north stair and portal, readable public rooms, and the gold crown with an asymmetric Sower. The final dome is brighter, the south gallery continues the cross-axis, and the new office view shows the partition and desk.

The model is accepted as a Minecraft architectural interpretation. Public-room relationships are based on the floor map, while furniture, offices, lighting, mosaics, sculpture, and murals are simplified. Lighting is functional and uses vanilla blocks; these images are actual game screenshots without shaders.

The NBT was then recaptured without placement to test the new MCP operation: all 16 images completed, the input SHA-256 stayed the same, and placement work remained zero. The final labeled images here are that recapture of the same iteration-3 geometry. The original placement manifest is retained separately.
