# Iteration 2 visual review

Reviewed all 14 Minecraft captures, including full-resolution Norris and rotunda views. The tower connection and courtyard enclosure fixed the first pass's most obvious exterior defects. Desk rows, galleries, the rotunda floor, and the upper chamber were now visible.

The ceiling above the rotunda still needed more useful light and a calmer tile pattern. The south cross-axis looked unfinished beyond the rotunda, and the Governor's Suite read as a single long room. The crown's Sower silhouette needed an asymmetric pose.

Decision: refine the dome lighting and abstract figurative panels, extend the vaulted south gallery, partition the office from reception, add two verification cameras, and refine the statue and entrance flags.
