# Iteration 1 visual review

Reviewed the 14 Minecraft PNGs and contact sheet after the first NBT placement. The base-to-tower proportions and four-courtyard plan read clearly in the aerial views.

Problems that drove iteration 2: a gap under the tower made it appear to float; courtyard elevations had floor bands and glazing without continuous walls; the Great Hall vault had open seams; the rotunda mosaic was difficult to see; Norris, Warner, and Supreme Court were too dark; a service-core wall obstructed the Memorial Chamber camera.

Decision: connect the tower to the roof, enclose courtyard walls, cover the vaults, put light at useful heights, and clear the upper room. Keep the same camera positions to compare those fixes.
